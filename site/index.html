Mission

Make every shop floor in India as intelligent as the products it builds.

Indian manufacturing runs on hand-written registers, mental math, and WhatsApp threads. We replace that with a single source of truth — scanned, tracked, and acted on in real time.

Vision

A factory where every component knows itself — and tells you where it's going.

Barcodes. Sensors. Edge devices. Cloud. Models. The whole chain wired together so the floor tells the office what's about to break before it does.

Platform

One platform.
The entire shop floor.

Live material movement. BOM-driven planning. HR & ERP. Floor automation. All wired to the same operational backbone so the data finally reconciles.

Live material tracking

Per-station Raspberry Pis scan barcodes on the floor. Stock, traceability, and quality events flow into the same ledger — no batch uploads, no end-of-day sync.

  • Barcode + QR + IoT scans
  • Real-time stock per SKU
  • Full part-to-cooker traceability

Production & planning

Bills-of-materials, vendor lead times, scrap factors. The platform tells you whether you can make 100 cookers next Friday before you commit to the order.

  • BOM-driven MRP
  • Feasibility & shortage analysis
  • Auto-suggested purchase orders

HR, ERP & automation

Attendance, payroll, vendor profiles, low-stock alerts over email, Pi fleet OTA. The back office and the floor finally use the same system.

  • Roles, audit log, alerts
  • Vendor + quality scorecards
  • Pi fleet provisioning via Ansible
