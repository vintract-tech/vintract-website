Mission

Bring Industry 4.0 to SMB manufacturers, without an SAP licence.

Mid-sized factories still run on hand-written registers, group chats and generic accounting tools. Enterprise ERPs assume a CFO and a six-figure implementation budget. Vintract closes that gap with low-cost industrial automation: the same operational backbone at one-tenth the price, deployed in weeks not years.

Vision

Every part on every line, scanned, traced and ledgered in real time.

The shop floor becomes a queryable system: every barcode scan, every component substitution, every reject. AI models trained on that ledger surface shortages before they happen and anomalies before they ship.

What We Build

The full stack.
Floor to forecast.

Whatever your plant needs, we build it on a proven stack: edge devices on the stations, FastAPI backend, Postgres ledger, web and mobile apps, AWS-native infrastructure. One team, one bill, one support line.

Live material tracking

Per-station Raspberry Pis publish barcode scans to AWS IoT Core; a Lambda writes to Postgres in under 500 ms. Stock, traceability and quality events all hit the same ledger. No batch sync, no end-of-day reconciliation.

  • Barcode + QR + IoT scans
  • Real-time on-hand per SKU
  • Full part-to-finished-good lineage

BOM-driven production

Define products and their bills-of-materials once. Feasibility check explodes the BOM against live stock and vendor lead times before you commit to an order: "100 cookers by Friday? 200 stainless discs short, 21-day lead."

  • Bills of materials with scrap factor
  • Feasibility & shortage analysis
  • Vendor lead-time aware ETA

Vendors, waste & reports

Vendor profiles with quality scorecards, waste log against specific SKUs and production orders, XLSX-exportable reports with charts. Low-stock email alerts with role-based recipient lists.

  • Roles, audit log, email alerts
  • Vendor quality scorecards
  • Pi fleet provisioning via Ansible

FAQ

Straight answers.

What does Industry 4.0 cost for a small factory?

A fraction of an enterprise ERP. Vintract runs on lean cloud infrastructure, you pay only for what we build for your plant, and most deployments go live for less than the annual maintenance fee of a legacy system. Ask us for a quote sized to your plant.

Can we start with one workflow instead of a full ERP?

Yes. Hyundai Transys India started with digital autonomous maintenance check sheets alone. Pick the workflow that hurts most, digitise it, and expand only if it earns its keep.

How long does deployment take?

Weeks, not years. A focused workflow ships in about three weeks. A full ERP rollout, including IoT scanning stations on the floor, typically lands in about six weeks.
