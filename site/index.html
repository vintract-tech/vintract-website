Mission

Bring Industry 4.0 to SMB manufacturers — without an SAP licence.

Mid-sized factories still run on hand-written registers, group chats and generic accounting tools. Enterprise ERPs assume a CFO and a six-figure implementation budget. Vintract closes that gap: same operational backbone, one-tenth the price, deployed in weeks not years.

Vision

Every part on every line, scanned, traced and ledgered in real time.

The shop floor becomes a queryable system: every barcode scan, every component substitution, every reject. AI models trained on that ledger surface shortages before they happen and anomalies before they ship.

Platform

The full stack.
Floor to forecast.

Edge devices on every station, FastAPI backend, Postgres ledger, Next.js web app, React Native mobile, AWS-native infrastructure. One vendor, one bill, one support line.

Live material tracking

Per-station Raspberry Pis publish barcode scans to AWS IoT Core; a Lambda writes to Postgres in under 500 ms. Stock, traceability and quality events all hit the same ledger — no batch sync, no end-of-day reconciliation.

  • Barcode + QR + IoT scans
  • Real-time on-hand per SKU
  • Full part-to-finished-good lineage

BOM-driven production

Define products and their bills-of-materials once. Feasibility check explodes the BOM against live stock + vendor lead times before you commit to an order — "100 cookers by Friday: 200 stainless discs short, 21-day lead".

  • Bills of materials with scrap factor
  • Feasibility & shortage analysis
  • Vendor lead-time aware ETA

Vendors, waste & reports

Vendor profiles with quality scorecards, waste log against specific SKUs and production orders, XLSX-exportable reports with charts. Low-stock email alerts with role-based recipient lists.

  • Roles, audit log, email alerts
  • Vendor quality scorecards
  • Pi fleet provisioning via Ansible
Mobile

A universal Android app for the operator on the floor.

One binary, many factories. The operator types a workspace code at login and the app resolves their company's backend automatically. No app-per-customer rebuild.

  • Scan an SKU — see live stock, vendor, threshold, history.
  • Receive inward and move stock to floor with scan + qty.
  • Production orders: start, save progress, mark complete.
  • Waste log with reason picker — automatically decrements stock.

How we deploy

Weeks, not years.

A scoped, fixed-fee rollout. From kickoff to first scan in 8–14 weeks for a single-site factory.

01

Discovery

SKUs, stations, BOMs, integrations. We walk the floor and scope the work.

02

Provision

03
